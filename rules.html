Community rules

The Board is for NTU students to find each other's businesses, societies and events. Keep it useful, honest and respectful.

The short version: be real, be decent, and don't post anything you wouldn't say to someone's face on campus.

1. No offensive language

Listings and reviews can't contain swearing, slurs or insults. Posts with offensive words are blocked automatically and won't go up.

2. No hate, harassment or threats

  • No content attacking anyone for their race, religion, nationality, sex, sexuality, gender, disability or background.
  • No bullying, threats, or targeting a specific person.
  • No extremist symbols, slogans or groups.

3. Real listings only

  • Only list a business, society or event you actually run or represent.
  • Use real, working contact details.
  • Describe your prices and services honestly.
  • No fake, joke or spam listings.

4. Honest reviews

  • Only review a business you've genuinely used.
  • One review per person per business.
  • Don't review your own business, or ask friends to leave fake reviews.
  • Criticism is fine. Abuse isn't.

5. Nothing illegal or unsafe

  • No drugs, weapons, alcohol or tobacco sales.
  • No adult or sexual content or services.
  • No selling essays or doing other people's coursework.
  • No scams, pyramid schemes or anything breaking UK law.

6. No politics or trolling

The Board isn't the place for political campaigning, arguments or posts made just to provoke people. Societies can describe what they do, but listings shouldn't be used to push an agenda.

7. Stay safe

  • Keep chats on the Board where you can.
  • Never share card or bank details in messages.
  • Meet in public places on or near campus.

How we check posts

Every listing and review is checked automatically when it's posted. Posts that clearly break these rules are blocked. Posts that might break them are held until our team reviews them. You can also report anything that slips through using the Report link on any listing or review.

What happens if you break the rules

We may remove your listing or review without warning. Repeated or serious breaks can lead to your account being removed.

The Board is a listings platform only. We don't vet, endorse or take responsibility for anyone's product or service. Not an official NTU or NTSU service.
