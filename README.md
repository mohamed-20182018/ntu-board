# the Board

One place for NTU students to find student-run businesses, societies, and official uni news — instead of digging through five different Instagram pages and group chats.

## What's in it

- **Student Services** — small student businesses (hair, nails, tutoring, bike repairs, whatever) list what they do, their prices, and how to book them
- **Societies** — what's on, when it meets, how to join
- **Official Adverts** — space reserved for NTU/NTSU to put out real announcements

Free to list. Always.

## Where it's at right now

Still early days — this is a working demo, not the finished product. Businesses and societies are being added by hand for now while we sort out a proper way for people to register themselves and have that data flow straight into the site, instead of someone manually typing it in every time.

## Built by

A small team of NTU students — some of us coding it, some of us handling the outreach and business side. Started as one idea, now an actual team effort.

## Running it locally

Right now it's just a single `index.html` file — clone the repo and open it in a browser. No build step, no dependencies, nothing complicated.

## Status

Early-stage and moving fast. Things will change, break, and get rebuilt as we go — that's expected at this point, not a sign something's wrong.
