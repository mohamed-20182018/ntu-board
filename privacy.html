Privacy notice

What we collect, why, who can see it, and how to have it removed. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Who we are

The Board is run by DAXSintech, Nottingham, UK. We are the “controller” of your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Contact us

Email daxsintech@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days, and within one month for data requests.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, the name you give us, whether you joined as a student or a business, and your password. Passwords are stored by our database provider in scrambled (hashed) form. We can’t see them.
  • Listings you post: the name, category, description, where and when, price list, policies, contact details (for example an Instagram handle or email) and any pictures you add. These are public once they’re live.
  • Reviews you post: your star rating, what you wrote, and your name as it appears on your account. These are public once they’re live.
  • Messages: what you write in a chat, when you sent it, and when the other person read it. Only you and the person you’re talking to can read a chat. While you’re in a chat, the other person can see that you’re there and when you’re typing, but this isn’t stored. When a student starts a chat, the business or society sees the name on their account.
  • Reports you send: what you reported and why. Only our team can see reports. The person you reported can’t see who sent it.
  • Technical information: our hosting and database providers keep short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure.

We don’t use analytics or advertising trackers and we don’t sell data. Our fonts and code are served from our own site.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • Running your account and showing what you post: to provide the service you signed up for (contract).
  • Confirming your email and resetting your password: we email you a code. This is part of providing the service.
  • Checking listings and reviews, handling reports and stopping spam: our legitimate interest in keeping the Board accurate and safe. Text you post is checked automatically, and our team looks at anything flagged and at every photo.

Who can see what

  • Anyone can see live listings and reviews, including the contact details and pictures in them.
  • Your email address is never shown on the site.
  • Messages can only be read by the two people in the chat. Our team doesn’t read them as part of normal running of the site.
  • Listings and reviews waiting for approval can only be seen by you and our team.

Companies that process data for us

  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Supabase: stores accounts, listings, reviews, messages, reports and pictures, and sends sign-up and password emails ([your Supabase project region, from Supabase → Settings → General]).

These may be US companies. Where data leaves the UK it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires (the UK–US data bridge or the UK’s approved contract clauses).

How long we keep it

Your account and what you post stay until you delete them. You can remove a listing or review, or delete your whole account, at any time from the site. Deleting your account removes your listings, reviews and messages too. Reports are deleted once they’ve been dealt with. We may remove anything that breaks our terms or community rules.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, get a copy of it, or object to how we use it. Email daxsintech@gmail.com. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and browser storage

The Board doesn’t set any cookies. It keeps two things in your browser’s storage, which stay on your device:

NameWhat it does
board-themeRemembers light or dark mode, if you chose one.
sb-…-auth-tokenKeeps you logged in. Removed when you log out.

Both are needed for the site to work the way you asked, so we don’t ask for consent. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

Age

The Board is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change this notice we’ll update the date at the top.
