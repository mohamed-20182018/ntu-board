How the Board works

Find a student business, check what they charge and how they work, message them, then review them once your appointment is done. Running something yourself? List it for free.

Looking for something

Hair, nails, food, tutoring, photos, repairs and more, all run by students.

  1. Pick a categoryChoose a category, then the type you need, like Barbers or Braids. Search and sort from there.
  2. Check the profileSee prices, photos, reviews and how fast they usually reply. Tap the highlights for late fees and policies.
  3. Message themPick what you want from their menu and it goes into a message with the total. Chats stay on the Board.
  4. Review after your appointmentOnce the business marks your appointment complete, you can leave one review.

Running something

A side hustle, a society, or an official notice from a university or students’ union. Free to list, always.

  1. Make a business accountSign up and choose “a student running a business”, or switch from your student account in your dashboard.
  2. Add your listingYour menu and prices, policies, a profile picture, a cover picture and up to four photos of your work.
  3. Reply to customersMessages land in your inbox. Your average reply time shows on your profile, so quick replies help.
  4. Mark appointments completeDo it from the chat. It lets that customer leave you a review, and your dashboard keeps count.

Why you can trust the reviews

Only students who have had an appointment can leave a review, and only one per business. The business marks the appointment complete, not the customer, so reviews come from real visits.

Staying safe

  • Keep chats on the Board so there is a record.
  • Never send card or bank details in a message.
  • Use Report in any chat if something feels wrong.
  • We list businesses, we don't vet them. Check reviews and photos before you book.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Browsing, messaging and listing are all free.

Who can list?

Any student running a business or a society, plus university and students’ union staff for official notices. You need a business account to list.

Is this run by a university?

No. The Board is made by students. It is not an official university or students’ union service, and it is open to students from any university.

Can I review a business I haven't used?

No. Reviews unlock once the business marks your appointment complete, so every review comes from a real customer.

Can I have a student account and a business?

Yes. A business account can still message other businesses. Use “My enquiries” in Messages to see those chats.

How do I change or remove my listing?

Email us at hello@theboard.example for now. Editing from your dashboard is coming.
