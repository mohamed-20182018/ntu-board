Running something

A side hustle, a small business, or a society. Free to list, always.

  1. Make a free accountSign up with your email. We send you a code to confirm it’s really you.
  2. Add your listingWhat you offer, where and when, how to reach you, your price list and policies, a profile picture and up to four photos.

How reviews work

You need to be logged in to leave a review, and you can leave one per business. You can’t review your own business. Every review is checked automatically, and anyone can report a review that breaks the community rules.

Featured and sponsored spots

The spotlight on the home page shows Featured businesses, which are the best rated ones for the category you picked. If a business has paid to appear there it is always marked Sponsored. Paying never changes a business’s reviews, rating or position in search.

Staying safe

  • We list businesses, we don’t vet them. Check reviews and photos before you book.
  • Be careful about paying in full up front to someone you haven’t used before.
  • Meet in public or on campus where you can.
  • Use Report on any listing or review if something feels wrong.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Browsing, reviewing and listing are all free.

Can I message a business on the Board?

Not yet. Use the contact details on their listing. You can copy them with one tap.

What does “Sponsored” mean?

The business paid to be shown in the spotlight. Everything else, including search results, ratings and reviews, is the same for every business.

Who can list?

Any student aged 16 or over running a business or a society. You need a free account.

Is this run by a university?

No. The Board is made by students and run by DAXSintech. It is not an official university or students’ union service.

Why is my listing “waiting for approval”?

Either the automatic check flagged something, or you added photos. Our team takes a quick look and approves it, usually within a day.

How do I delete my account or get a copy of my data?

Log in, open your profile and use “Delete account”. It removes your account and everything you’ve posted. For a copy of your data, email daxsintech@gmail.com.
