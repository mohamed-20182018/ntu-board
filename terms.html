We only list businesses and societies. Any booking, payment or service is agreed directly between you and them. We are not part of that agreement and we don’t guarantee any business.

Your account

  • You must be 16 or over to make an account.
  • Use your own email address and keep your password to yourself. You’re responsible for what’s posted from your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your profile.

Posting a listing

  • The business or society must be yours, or you must be allowed to list it.
  • Everything you post must be accurate, legal and yours to share. Contact details must be real.
  • Listings are checked automatically and some, including any with photos, are checked by our team before they appear. We can refuse or remove any listing at any time.
  • You must not pretend to be a university, a students’ union or anyone else.

Reviews

  • Only review a business you’ve actually used, and be honest. One review per business.
  • You can’t review your own business, or ask or pay anyone for a review.
  • We can remove reviews that break the community rules.

What you can’t do

  • Post anything illegal, age-restricted, hateful, sexual or misleading.
  • Post spam, links to scams, or other people’s personal details.
  • Try to break, overload or get around the security of the site.

Businesses

You must follow the law that applies to your business, including consumer, food hygiene and tax rules, and keep your prices and policies accurate.

Sponsored spots

Businesses that pay to appear in the home page spotlight are always marked “Sponsored”. Paying doesn’t change reviews, ratings or search results.
